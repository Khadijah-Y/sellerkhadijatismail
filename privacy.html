Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This website belongs to Khadijat Ismail, a freelance book publishing specialist. This page explains what information is collected when you visit the site or contact me, and how it is used.

Information you choose to send me

When you contact me by WhatsApp, email, Upwork, Fiverr or LinkedIn, I receive the details you include, such as your name, contact details, information about your book and any files you share. The quote form on this site does not store anything: it simply opens WhatsApp or your email app with your message filled in, and you decide whether to send it.

I use this information only to reply to you, prepare a quote and work on your project.

Your manuscript and files

Manuscripts, images and other files you send me are treated as confidential. I use them only for your project, I do not share them with anyone without your permission, and I do not claim any rights to your work. I am happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before we start.

Information collected automatically

This site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools. Like most websites, the hosting provider (Vercel) may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to keep the site secure and running. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you open the site.

Links to other sites

This site links to WhatsApp, Upwork, LinkedIn, Instagram and other services. Their own privacy policies apply when you use them.

How long I keep information

I keep messages and project files only as long as needed to complete your project and handle any follow-up changes. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you. Just send me a message using the details below.
