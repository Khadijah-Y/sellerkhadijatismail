What I do

Everything your manuscript needs before it meets readers.

From the first proofread to the cover and the final print-ready file, so you can publish with confidence.

01

Book formatting & typesetting

Professional interior layout for paperback, hardcover and ebook, built to the requirements of Amazon KDP, IngramSpark and other platforms.

  • Trim size, margins, bleed & gutters
  • Chapter openers, headers & page numbers
  • Clickable contents for ebooks
  • Print PDF + EPUB / Kindle files
02

Proofreading

A careful final check of your manuscript for spelling, grammar, punctuation, typos and small consistency issues before publication.

  • Spelling, grammar & punctuation
  • Typos and repeated words
  • Consistent names, numbers & style
  • Tracked changes you approve
03

Copy editing

Clearer, smoother sentences. I improve structure, clarity, readability, grammar and consistency while keeping your voice and your meaning.

  • Sentence flow and clarity
  • Grammar and word choice
  • Consistency across chapters
  • Your voice, kept intact
04

Book cover design

Covers that look at home next to bestsellers in your genre, designed to stand out as a tiny thumbnail and on the shelf.

  • Ebook front cover
  • Full print wrap: front, spine & back
  • Exact KDP & IngramSpark sizing
  • 3D mockups for your launch
05

Book translation

Bring your book to new readers in another language. I translate the text and rebuild the layout, so the new edition is ready to publish.

  • Natural, faithful translation
  • Original formatting kept
  • Contents and headings matched
  • Print and ebook ready

Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Send me a message, I reply personally.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your manuscript (Word or Google Docs), the trim size if you know it, the formats you need (paperback, hardcover, ebook), any images, and your target launch date.

Which platforms will my files work on?

Files are prepared to the requirements of Amazon KDP and IngramSpark, and can also be used on platforms such as Apple Books, Barnes & Noble Press, Kobo, Lulu and Draft2Digital.

What types of books do you work on?

Mainly nonfiction, self-help and personal development, children's books, workbooks, journals, educational materials and practical guides. I also consider fiction and other genres depending on the manuscript.

What's the difference between proofreading and copy editing?

Proofreading is the final check for spelling, grammar, punctuation and typos. Copy editing goes deeper: it improves sentence structure, clarity and consistency while keeping your voice.

Do I keep the rights to my book?

Yes. Your manuscript and every file I deliver belong to you.
