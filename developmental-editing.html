See the big picture of your book

Before sentences are polished, the structure has to work. Developmental editing looks at your whole manuscript: how it's organised, how it flows and whether it delivers what it promises. I take on a small number of projects within my areas of expertise.

What I offer

What developmental editing includes

Available as an add-on to other services or on its own, for selected projects.

01

Manuscript assessment

A read of your full manuscript with a written report on its strengths and what to improve.

02

Structure and flow

Advice on chapter order, pacing, transitions and anything that slows the reader down.

03

Chapter notes

Comments in the manuscript where specific sections need work.

04

Follow-up questions

A chance to ask questions about the report before you start revising.

Quality

Why start with the big picture

Fix the foundations first

It's better to fix structure before paying for line edits on chapters that may change.

A clear plan

You get specific, practical notes instead of a vague feeling that something isn't working.

Honest and kind

Feedback that's direct about what to change and clear about what already works.

Within my expertise

I only accept projects I can genuinely help with, mainly nonfiction, self-help and guides.

The details

What I look at

Structure

  • Chapter order and logic
  • Opening and ending
  • Pacing across the book
  • Gaps and repetition
  • Promise to the reader
  • Front and back matter

Flow

  • Transitions between chapters
  • Argument or story arc
  • Character or example development
  • Reader engagement
  • Tone and audience
  • Priorities for revision

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Send me a message.

Why "selected projects"?

I only accept projects within my expertise, so I can give genuinely useful feedback. Send me your synopsis and I'll tell you honestly if it's a good fit.

Will you rewrite my book?

No. Developmental editing gives you a plan and notes. You do the revising, and I can help with copy editing afterwards.

What do I receive?

A written editorial report plus comments in your manuscript, as agreed in your quote.

Can I add other services?

Yes. Developmental editing can be followed by copy editing, proofreading, formatting and a cover.
