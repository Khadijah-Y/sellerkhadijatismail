Quality

Why proofreading matters

First impressions

Readers notice typos quickly, and reviews often mention them. A clean book earns more trust.

Fresh eyes

After months with your manuscript, your brain reads what you meant to write. A proofreader reads what's really there.

Your voice stays yours

Proofreading fixes errors only. Your style, word choices and meaning stay as you wrote them.

You stay in control

Every change is tracked, so you can accept or reject each one.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Send me a message.

What's the difference between proofreading and copy editing?

Proofreading is the final check for spelling, grammar, punctuation and typos. Copy editing goes deeper and improves sentences for clarity and flow.

Will you change my writing style?

No. Proofreading corrects errors only. If a sentence is unclear, I leave a comment instead of rewriting it.

How will I see the corrections?

In Word or Google Docs with tracked changes, so you can see, accept or reject every correction.

Which English do you use?

American, British or Canadian English. Tell me which one, or I'll follow the style your manuscript already uses.
